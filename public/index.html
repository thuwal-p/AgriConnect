About AgriConnect

What can you expect?

AgriConnect is designed to simplify the connection between agricultural producers and markets through crop management, mandi discovery and data-driven recommendations.

01

For farmers

Manage crop details, find mandis that accept your produce and view ranked recommendations.

02

For mandis

Manage mandi information, accepted crops, buying prices and available capacity.

03

Smarter recommendations

Compare suitable mandis using crop and market information. Recommendations currently rank mandis by crop compatibility, available capacity and expected selling value.
